The Practice

Architecture as
civic responsibility.

We believe buildings are arguments. Arguments about how we inhabit land, how we relate to one another, how we mark time. Every project begins with a question about what the building needs to say — and ends only when that argument is resolved, in material, in light, in space.

Our practice is intentionally small. We take on fewer projects so that each one receives our full attention. We work with clients who understand that good architecture takes time, and that the result is worth it.

Adam Chen
Principal Architect — AIBC Registered

Adam founded Prism in 2009 after working with practices in Vancouver and Zürich. His work focuses on the relationship between building and landscape, particularly in the complex terrain of the North Shore.
