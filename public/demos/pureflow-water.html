Process

Simple as Water Should Be

From mountain spring to your front door — a closed-loop system designed for zero friction and zero waste.

01

Choose Your Plan

Select from weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly delivery. Pick the jug sizes that fit your household or office — 5L or 18.9L available.

02

We Deliver to Your Door

Fresh BC spring water arrives on your scheduled day. Our drivers are trained, punctual, and will leave jugs at your preferred spot.

03

We Pick Up Your Empties

Leave empties out on delivery day. We collect, sanitize, and refill them — a fully closed loop with zero landfill waste.
