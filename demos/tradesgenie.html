Who we are

Your renovation, without the chaos.

TradesGenie Contracting is a Vancouver-based general contractor specializing in condo, kitchen, bathroom and whole-home renovations across the Lower Mainland. We earned our reputation the hard way — one organized, on-budget project at a time.

From the first walkthrough to the final clean-up, you get one accountable point of contact, a clear fixed-price estimate, and a crew that respects your home, your strata and your timeline.

  • Upfront, itemized estimates — no surprises mid-build
  • Strata & permit coordination handled for you
  • Clean job sites and proactive weekly updates
