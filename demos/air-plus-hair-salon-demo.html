The Salon

A calmer kind of
chair time.

Air Plus is a full-service hair studio inside Metrotown, built around one idea: your hair should feel as good as it looks. Every visit starts with a real consultation — we read your hair, your routine and the look you're after before a single section is clipped.

From precision cuts and lived-in colour to perms, keratin and scalp therapy, our directors work with premium, low-ammonia products and the techniques to match. Quiet music, good coffee, no rush.
