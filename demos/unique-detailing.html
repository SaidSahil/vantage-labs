4Step process
How we work

From first look to a finish that lasts.

1

Inspection & Consultation

We assess your paint, walk through your goals and recommend the right level of correction and protection — no pressure.

2

Decontamination & Wash

A safe multi-stage wash, clay-bar and chemical decontamination strips away everything bonded to the surface.

3

Correction & Prep

Machine polishing removes swirls and defects, then the surface is prepped so coatings bond perfectly.

4

Coating & Final Inspection

We apply your ceramic, PPF or wax, then a detailed final inspection and handover under studio lighting.
