Who we are

We build it like it’s our own home.

Founded in 2017, Lower Coast Building Group is a North Vancouver–based Certified General Contractor specializing in quality custom home renovations, additions and new builds across the Lower Mainland and Sea-to-Sky.

Our promise is simple: full transparency, honesty & integrity on every project — and craftsmanship built to last for life, not just to look good on handover day. Quality isn’t a feature here. It’s the whole focus.

  • Certified General Contractor — licensed, insured & accountable
  • Transparent, itemized estimates — no hidden surprises
  • One dedicated point of contact from first walk-through to handover
